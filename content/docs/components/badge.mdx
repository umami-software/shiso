---
title: Badge
---

Use `Badge` for short inline labels such as status or version markers.

## Supported props

- `tone?: 'muted' | 'primary'` (defaults to `'muted'`)
- `children?: ReactNode`

## Basic usage

```tsx
<Badge>beta</Badge> <Badge tone="primary">new</Badge>
```

<Badge>beta</Badge> <Badge tone="primary">new</Badge>

## In headings

```tsx
### Streaming responses <Badge tone="primary">new</Badge>
```

### Streaming responses <Badge tone="primary">new</Badge>

## Behavior notes

- `ParamField` and `ResponseField` use the same badge for their `required` and `deprecated` markers
