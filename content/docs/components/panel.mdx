---
title: Panel
description: Pin supplementary content to the right rail.
---

Use `Panel` to replace the page's table of contents with supplementary
content: examples, callouts, or anything else. On small screens the content
renders inline where it is written.

<Panel>
  <Info>This panel replaces the table of contents on desktop.</Info>
</Panel>

```tsx
<Panel>
  <Info>Pin info to the side panel. Or add any other component.</Info>
</Panel>
```

Only one `Panel` per page is supported; the last one wins.

## Properties

<PropertiesTable>
<PropertiesTable.Row name="children" type="ReactNode">
  Content pinned to the right rail on desktop.
</PropertiesTable.Row>
</PropertiesTable>
